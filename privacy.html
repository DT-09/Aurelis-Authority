AURELIS / PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

This notice describes the information collected when someone submits an enquiry through the Aurelis Authority website or uses its public trace-audit workspace.

Do not submit sensitive information. The enquiry form is not a secure file-transfer channel. Do not include credentials, secrets, production customer records, payment data or raw trace exports.

Enquiry information

If you submit the enquiry form, the site stores your name, email address, optional company and role, the type of team you represent, a short workflow description, your stated timing, trace-export readiness, the page/source label and submission time. The information is used to review and respond to that enquiry.

Website measurement

The site records aggregate daily counts for page views, trace-workspace visits, successful trace audits, enquiry-form opens and accepted enquiries. Source labels may be derived from a campaign parameter in the page URL. This measurement does not use advertising cookies, store IP addresses, or record the contents of trace audits. Counts are not unique-user counts.

Service providers

Enquiry records are stored in Cloudflare D1. If email notification is enabled, the notification is sent through Resend using the configured account. These providers process information to operate the service. No trace policy or trace contents are included in the notification.

Trace workspace

Trace policy and event data submitted to the public audit endpoint are evaluated for that request and are not stored by the audit endpoint. The tool returns results based only on the records supplied. Do not submit secrets or personal data.

Retention and requests

Enquiry records are retained only as long as needed to assess and respond to the enquiry and to maintain appropriate business records. To request access, correction or deletion of an enquiry record, contact dhairytopia@gmail.com and include the email address used for the enquiry. Do not send sensitive identity documents by email.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the website's data handling changes. Check the date of the latest repository update for the current version.
