## Send discipline

1. Send only after reviewing the recipient and final wording.
2. Do not claim that Aurelis has customer deployments, certifications, live integrations or proven security outcomes.
3. Do not attach customer data or ask for credentials. First request a short scoping conversation; only later agree a sanitized staging sample and written authorization.
4. Do not provide free bespoke assessments. The first engagement should be a paid, fixed-scope pilot.
5. Do not sign a contract, accept payment, or make legal/tax representations without an adult-authorized and legally appropriate arrangement.

## Qualification outcome

Advance a prospect only if it confirms:
- It has an agent that can take consequential actions.
- It has an explicit policy or can define one.
- It can export sanitized staging traces.
- It has a current deployment or release where the review is useful.
- A buyer can approve a paid pilot.

If those conditions are absent, do not spend engineering time building a custom adapter for that prospect.
